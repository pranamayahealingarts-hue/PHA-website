Practical Info

Session length:
Thai Massage: Sessions are 1hr 45min. For lasting results, I recommend 2–3 bodywork sessions with added personalised yoga.

Cancellation policy: Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you need to reschedule. Late cancellations will be charged at full rate.
