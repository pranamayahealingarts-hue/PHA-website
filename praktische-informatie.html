Practical Info

Location: Sessions take place in Whistler, BC. The exact address is provided upon booking confirmation.

Session length: Initial sessions are 90 minutes. Follow-up sessions are 60 or 90 minutes depending on what we are working on. Online consultations (Compassionate Inquiry Coaching) are available via video call.

Rates: Please contact me directly for current rates. Packages are available for those committing to a series of sessions.

What to wear: For Thai Massage and fascia mobility work, wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing — you will remain fully clothed. For breathwork sessions, comfortable layers are recommended.

Cancellation policy: Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you need to reschedule. Late cancellations may be charged at full rate.
