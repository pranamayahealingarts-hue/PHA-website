About

My Background

I work at the intersection of the body, the nervous system, and emotional wellbeing. With a background in Psychology and Neuroscience, I spent years figuring out whether I wanted to work with the mind, or the body. I was always looking for the area where the two came together.

That search led me beyond the world of academia and into the body itself. Seeing thousands of bodies with Thai massage showed me how injuries are never just local but affect the entire body, and how our emotional life is stored in the body. Over the years, I have trained in Traditional Thai Massage in Thailand and India, Compassionate Inquiry (Dr. Gabor Maté's framework), yoga, coaching and relaxation massage. Together, these disciplines have shaped a way of working that sees the body not as separate from the mind, but as an essential part of the healing process.

I don't fit neatly into a single wellness category — and that is precisely what makes this work so effective. Rather than treating symptoms in isolation, I look at the whole person: body, mind, emotions, and nervous system.

Many people arrive with a vague sense that their symptoms are not only physical, but aren't sure what to do with that. Others have been told their symptoms are just stress-related, or that they simply have to learn to live with them. My experience has shown me otherwise. The body is always communicating. Physical tension, pain, fatigue, anxiety, and emotional patterns are often deeply connected. When we learn to listen to the body's language, change becomes possible in places where conventional approaches often reach their limits.
