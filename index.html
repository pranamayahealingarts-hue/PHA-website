Body-Centred Transformation

I work at the intersection of the body, the nervous system, and emotional health. Most people come to me after they've tried everything else — physiotherapy, medication, talk therapy — and something is still not resolving. I work with the part those systems miss.

My Approach

Healing cannot happen without attention to the body. Your body knows your story. I invite you to slow down. To turn inward. To feel what your body is actually telling you — not what you think it should be saying.

Together, we explore what is blocking you from living at full capacity. Through intuitive touch, breathwork, and compassionate inquiry, I help you reconnect with what your body needs. Most people arrive here after years of being told their symptoms have no cause. They do. The body simply speaks in a language most practitioners haven't learned to hear.

I hold a Bachelor's in Psychology and a Master's in Neuroscience. I have spent years studying what happens in the body when conventional medicine reaches its limits — and what becomes possible when we stop separating the physical from the emotional.

My training spans Traditional Thai Massage (Chiang Mai, Thailand), Compassionate Inquiry (Dr. Gabor Maté's framework), yoga, breathwork, and fascia mobility work. I don't fit neatly into any single wellness category — and that is precisely what makes this work extraordinary.
