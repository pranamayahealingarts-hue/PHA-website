Vision

Most people living with chronic illness have spent years — sometimes decades — moving from one specialist to the next. Each one asking the same question: how do we get rid of these symptoms?

That question, however well-intentioned, misses something fundamental. Symptoms are not the problem. They are the body's attempt to communicate something that hasn't yet been heard. And when we spend ten years trying to silence that signal, the body does what any messenger would do when ignored — it speaks louder.

My work starts from a different premise: the body is not broken. It is trying to tell you something. When we stop fighting the symptoms and start listening to what they're pointing to — the nervous system can finally settle, the pattern can complete, and the body no longer needs to shout.

This doesn't mean chronic illness is "just in your head." It means the path out may run through the body rather than around it.

What I Do

I work with the body directly — through traditional Thai massage, somatic coaching, and yoga. Depending on what you're carrying and what you're looking for, sessions may focus on physical release, emotional processing, or both at once.

In a Thai massage session, I work across the entire body — tracing tension back to its source, releasing tissue that has been guarding for years, and allowing the nervous system to finally settle. The results are often immediate and profound in a way that localised treatment rarely is.

In somatic coaching sessions, I create the conditions for physical processes to complete that the body has been suppressing — often for years. When a sensation is finally allowed to happen, the emotion or belief underneath it becomes accessible. The cycle that was once interrupted can now finish — and what the body was holding no longer needs to be held.

In both cases, the entry point is the body. That is where the work lives.

Who Is This For?

Most people who find their way here have already tried a lot of things — and are exhausted from trying. They've seen the specialists, done the therapy, followed the advice. Some progress, maybe. But something is still there, still unresolved, still showing up in the body in one form or another.

It shows up as emotional patterns that years of talk therapy have circled without fully resolving. As a grief that was pushed through rather than moved through. As anxiety that lives in the chest, not the mind. As a body that feels older, stiffer, more disconnected than it should.

In all of these cases, the body is not the problem. It is carrying something that was never given the space to complete. And until it is — the pattern continues.

My work is for anyone ready to stop working around the body, and start working with it.
