Training & Credentials

Academic: Bachelor of Science in Psychology — Master of Science in Neuroscience. My academic background forms the scientific foundation for every session: an understanding of how the brain, nervous system, and body interact, and what the research actually shows about healing.

Thai Massage: Certified in Traditional Thai Massage in Chiang Mai, Thailand, with advanced Masters-level training in India. Chiang Mai is the historical heartland of the tradition; my training there was rigorous, long-form, and lineage-based.

Compassionate Inquiry: Trained in the Compassionate Inquiry framework developed by Dr. Gabor Maté — a psychotherapeutic approach that uncovers the unconscious beliefs and emotional patterns driving physical and psychological symptoms.

Yoga: Certified yoga teacher since 2015, trained in Nicaragua. Teaching yoga taught me how to hold space, work with breath, and guide people into embodied awareness — skills that thread through every modality I practise.

Swedish & Spa Massage: Trained and worked at a luxury spa in Whistler, BC. This gave me the technical precision and client-centred professionalism that underpins all hands-on sessions.

Breathwork & Fascia Mobility: Ongoing specialist training in breathwork facilitation and fascial release techniques.
