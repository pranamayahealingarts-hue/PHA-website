Training & Credentials

Swedish & Spa Massage: Trained and worked at a luxury spa in Whistler, BC. This gave me the technical precision and client-centred professionalism that underpins all hands-on sessions.

Breathwork & Fascia Mobility: Ongoing specialist training in breathwork facilitation and fascial release techniques.
