Fascia Mobility Work

Fascia is the connective tissue web that surrounds every muscle, organ, nerve, and bone in the body. When it becomes restricted through injury, chronic stress, or sedentary patterns, it creates compression, pain, and a body that cannot move — or heal — freely.

Fascia mobility work uses slow, sustained pressure and movement to restore elasticity and circulation to the connective tissue. Unlike stretching, which targets muscle, this work goes into the tissue itself. The results include improved range of motion, reduced chronic pain, better fluid circulation, and a nervous system that finally feels safe enough to let go.
